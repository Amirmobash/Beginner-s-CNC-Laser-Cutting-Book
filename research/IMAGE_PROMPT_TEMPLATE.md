# Per-figure master prompt template

Copy to `assets/image_prompts/FXX_MASTER_PROMPT.md` (git-ignored, private) and fill every section **for that figure only**. Never reuse a generic prompt. Dimensions must be read from the generator, not typed from memory.

1. Educational objective — what the German beginner must understand (one sentence).
2. Source material — chapter, source files, engineering constraints.
3. Verified geometry — dimensions, proportions, joint details, with the generator output they came from.
4. Image type — authentic photograph | photorealistic CAD render | semi-realistic technical illustration | stylized educational illustration | genuine software screenshot | exact technical diagram.
5. Scene description.
6. Camera and view — projection or focal length, framing, visible components.
7. Material and texture.
8. Exact details that must be correct (parts, dimensions, software state).
9. Beginner usability — what is emphasised.
10. Lighting and visual quality.
11. Negative constraints — defects to avoid (extra parts, wrong joints, invented UI, fake test claims).
12. Output specification — resolution, aspect, colour space, format, filename, placement.
13. Verification criteria — what the engineer, illustrator and beginner reviewers inspect.

Rules: screenshots must be genuine and reproducible; generated/simulated images must not be presented as physical tests; German labels reviewed before approval.
