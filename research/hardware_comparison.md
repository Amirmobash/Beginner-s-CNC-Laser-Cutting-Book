## Required vendor evidence before recommendation

For **each exact revision**, record: manufacturer/manual URL+revision, German/EU seller and dated EUR price incl.VAT/shipping; usable XYZ/XY area; controller/firmware; spindle and laser accessory part numbers; documented3mm plywood support; approved enclosure and classification in intended use; interlock behavior; real emergency-stop/energy-isolation arrangements; approved exhaust path/filtration; dust extraction; replacement collets/tools/parts; beginner documentation; total accessory-inclusive cost.

Shared workshop/makerspace access is a sensible cost-reduction candidate for the first box, but prices, supervision, laser access and local safety arrangements must be verified with that workshop. It is not treated as free or universally available.

## Why no cheapest winner is named

An inexpensive open-frame laser is not a safe beginner setup merely by being inexpensive. Until a protective system and exact compatible materials are documented, machine operation remains blocked. The book's geometry and separate-process strategy can be developed without choosing a questionable low-price kit. The physical project must be supervised, tested and approved on a specific installation before commercial release.
