Birch/poplar plywood are candidate species, not a blanket laser approval: glue, coatings, density, voids and wavelength matter. Nominal3mm is not guaranteed actual3.000mm. Record multiple thickness measurements, use their spread in the design, and test kerf and joint fit before full panels. Do not force brittle charred fingers into undersized gaps. Obtain small quantities from a documented German/EU laser-material specialist or the approved machine supplier; no particular supplier or price has been verified.

Do not cut PVC, vinyl, unknown plastics or unknown coated panels. Do not substitute air fresheners, smell tests or open windows for approved extraction. Prices and total ownership cost are a remaining author/vendor research item, not a completed result.
