# Editorial package review

The package is a complete nine-chapter **working manuscript**, not a commercially released book. All chapters advance the same gift box, with du throughout and the named author supplied by the brief. Chapter-level reviews are in beginner_review.md, editorial_review.md and TECHNICAL_REVIEW.md. They are AI-assisted desk reviews, not independent human certification.

The former two-sign concept was replaced before manuscript distribution. Folder names and nominal dimensions were reconciled. Original diagrams are embedded, identified and captioned; none pretends to be a software screenshot or a manufactured result. A27-source bibliography is embedded in every edition. Source scope distinguishes development/archived documents from current releases.

Important limitations remain instructional rather than formatting defects: exact hardware and software/controller selection cannot be finalized from blocked manufacturer pages; full Windows GUI lessons, authentic screenshots, current euro prices, approved spindle/laser configurations and physical prototypes are unverified. The virtual CAM exercise now proves actual FreeCAD operation/simulation only. It does not supply a device-approved manufacturing lesson. The flush removable lid also needs a practical grip/usability check.

Genuine substantial author writing, engineering decisions, observed workshop results, beginner testing, rights, safety and publisher-specific review must precede commercial publication. No publisher upload, payment or public full-book distribution occurred.
